# Tap and Scrape Sequencer

This is my little browser music machine. It is going to play a looping set of taps and scrapes, all drawn as a small wooden rig with tapping arms.

Right now I'm on step 1 of 5: just the rig. There are four wooden arms hanging from a beam. Tap or click near an arm and it swings and knocks the board. Round tips are for taps, ridged tips are for scrapes.

Next I'll add beat slots, sounds, a looping play, and a tempo slider with a stop button.
