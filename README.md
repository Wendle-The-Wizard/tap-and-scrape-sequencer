# Tap and Scrape Sequencer

This is my little browser music machine. It is going to play a looping set of taps and scrapes, all drawn as a small wooden rig with tapping arms.

I'm on step 2 of 5 now: beat slots. Under the rig there are four rows of eight slots, one row for each arm. Tap a slot to switch it on (it glows) or off (it goes dark). Gold slots are taps and green ones are scrapes. You can still tap an arm to make it knock.

Next I'll add the sounds, a looping play, and a tempo slider with a stop button.
